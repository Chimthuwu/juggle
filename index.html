Our Manifesto

Keep
The
Balls
Flying.

We believe the circus belongs to everyone. But mainly to those who can juggle flaming torches while overthrowing the government.


Fascism thrives on fear, division, and people who drop the ball. We don't drop the ball. In fact, if we see a fascist, we will juggle their shoes, their lunchbox, and their preconceived notions of hierarchy.


We reject boring politics completely. We choose chaotic good. We choose unicycles. We choose throwing sharp objects in the air and catching them with our teeth while yelling anticapitalist slogans.


We juggle together, perform together, laugh together, and occasionally accidentally set our eyebrows on fire together. But it's worth it for the revolution.


Our demands are simple: 1. Free juggling clubs for all. 2. The immediate dissolution of all hierarchies (except for the human pyramid, which requires a strong base). 3. More glitter. If you disagree, prepare to face the wrath of 10,000 synchronized mimes.

Three
Principles.

A circus is built from many different skills, people and personalities. That's its strength.

Solidarity

We stand with people targeted by fascism, racism, bigotry and political violence. Nobody stands alone.
